# Voice for en-us (fictional example)

Register: direct, calm, second person.
Dialect or variant: American English spelling. Contractions are fine. No British spellings.
Sentence length: short. One idea per sentence.
How trust is built in this market: cost transparency first, then what happens at the first
visit, then reviews. Insurance questions come up early; answer them plainly.
Conversion action buyers actually take: online booking form for a first visit.

## Sounds right

1. You see the full cost before anything starts.
2. The X-ray happens at the same visit.
3. Book a consultation.

## Sounds wrong

1. We deliver comprehensive smile solutions with cutting-edge technology.
2. Denver's premier destination for your Hollywood smile.
3. Don't hesitate to contact us today!
