## problem
You message three clinics and each one says "come in first." After the visit you learn the X-ray is a second appointment and the final price is still unclear.

## specs
Clear aligners are a series of removable transparent trays, changed on a schedule set in your treatment plan. They suit mild to moderate cases; the dentist confirms this after the panoramic X-ray.

## proof
- A panoramic X-ray is taken during the first visit.
- You receive a written treatment plan with the full cost and duration before any treatment starts, and nothing begins until you approve it.

## how_it_works
1. Book online and pick a time.
2. The first visit takes about 45 minutes: exam and panoramic X-ray.
3. You receive the written treatment plan and decide at your own pace.

## objections
Will I know the total cost before I start? Yes. It is written in the treatment plan before any step.
Do I have to come back for the X-ray? No. It is taken in the same visit.
How long does it take? The duration is in your plan, and you see it before you begin.

## faq
Are clear aligners right for every case? No. Complex cases may need fixed braces; the dentist explains this after the X-ray.
Does the consultation commit me to treatment? No. You receive the plan and decide.
